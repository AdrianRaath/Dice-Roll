Roll the Dice Online

QuickDice is a free online dice roller that lets you roll virtual dice instantly in your browser. Roll up to 6 dice at once with support for all standard polyhedral dice - d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, and d100. No apps to download, no sign-ups required - just pick your dice, choose your sides, and roll.

Every roll uses cryptographically secure randomness to ensure completely unbiased results. Each die face has an equal probability of landing, just like rolling physical dice. Add positive or negative modifiers to any roll, track your results with built-in roll history, and roll again instantly with the spacebar.

How to Use Our Online Dice Roller

  1. Choose the number of dice - Select anywhere from 1 to 6 dice using the dropdown. Whether you need a single die roll or a handful of dice, we have you covered.
  2. Set the number of sides - Choose from d2 through d100. Classic board games use 6-sided dice (d6), while tabletop RPGs call for d4, d8, d10, d12, and d20.
  3. Add a modifier (optional) - Click the +/- button to add a positive or negative modifier to your roll. Perfect for D&D attack rolls, skill checks, and saving throws where you need to add a bonus.
  4. Hit Roll or press spacebar - Watch the dice tumble with a quick rolling animation. Each die lands independently with a completely random result, and the total appears instantly.

Every roll is recorded in the roll history below, showing individual dice values, modifiers, and totals. You can re-roll as many times as you like and change your dice configuration at any time. QuickDice works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Popular Use Cases

  • Board games and party games - Don't have physical dice handy? Use QuickDice as a replacement when playing Monopoly, Risk, Yahtzee, Catan, or any board game that requires dice. Our virtual dice roller is perfect for game nights when dice go missing.
  • Tabletop RPGs and D&D - Roll any standard polyhedral die from d4 to d100, add modifiers for attack rolls and skill checks, and track every result with roll history. QuickDice covers the full range of dice used in Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, and other tabletop RPGs.
  • Classroom activities and teaching - Teachers use online dice for probability lessons, maths games, and random student selection. A virtual dice roller is a simple, visual way to demonstrate chance and statistics to students of all ages.
  • Decision-making - Can't decide what to have for dinner or who goes first? Let the dice decide. Rolling dice online is a quick, fun, and impartial way to settle everyday choices and break ties.
  • Drinking games and icebreakers - Hosting a get-together? Pull up QuickDice on your phone and use it for any dice-based drinking game or party icebreaker - no physical set needed.
